Sinister Gauntlet (prototype)

Health: 100/100 Gold: 8 Weapon: Sword (1)
Goal: collect 30 gold, then press E at the purple square.
Enemy gold: goblins 0, skeletons 0. At 30 they summon a stronger boss.

WASD / arrows: move | Mouse: aim | Hold click / Space: attack

1: sword (straight) | 2: axe (wide) | 3: bow (1 gold/shot) | E: summon | P: pause | R: restart

How to play

1. Collect yellow gold. Break brown chests for more.

2. With 30 gold, return to the purple square in the middle. Press E.

3. Break the boss's 3 grey blocks, then hit its red center.

You are blue. Avoid monsters and red shots.

Collect yellow gold. Break brown chests for more.

Blue: you | Yellow: gold | Brown: chest | Grey: wall / boss part | Green & white: monsters | Red squares: enemy shots
