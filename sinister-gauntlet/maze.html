Goblin King maze

Health: 100/100 Gold: 0 Altar: 0/30 Left click: strike | King click: 5 gold
Goal: keep the altar under 30, then left click the king for 5 gold a hit.

WASD / arrows: move | Left click a monster or chest | Left click the king: 5 gold

P: pause | R: restart

How to play

1. Left click a gold chest to break it. Pick up the coins.

2. Goblins carry coins to the purple altar and can chip open chests. At 30, the Goblin King wakes.

3. Left click a monster to hit it. Left click the king to strike him for 5 gold.

You are blue. Skeletons shoot straight red arrows. Only the king smashes walls.

Break gold chests. Keep goblins from filling the altar.

Blue: you | Green: goblins | White: skeletons | Gold chest: coins | Purple: altar | Crowned green: Goblin King
